The place

A guingueta is a small bar by the water.
This one is ours.

Step off the Passeig Voramar and the pavement turns to sand. Kiwi Guingueta sits right where the beach begins — a thatched-roof bar with a terrace, a few sunbeds for hire, and a line of sight straight out to sea.

Come for a coffee in the morning, stay for a long lunch, and let the afternoon turn into cocktails. Children run about, dogs are welcome, and nobody hurries you along.

Open every day, 10:00 until midnight.
